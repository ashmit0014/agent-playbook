Agent Playbook

Prompts that make an AI work more carefully on real tasks. Choose what you are doing, copy the prompt, and paste it into your AI tool. The rules inside come from real projects that went wrong and were corrected. Nothing is installed: you see exactly what the AI receives.

Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini and other capable assistants. See the nine core principles.
