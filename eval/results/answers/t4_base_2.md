**Short answer: no measurable increase that I'd put in front of a board.** The best estimate is about **+2.5%** (roughly €5,500–5,900 over the 8 weeks since launch, about €700 a week), but that is within normal week-to-week noise, so we can't tell it apart from zero.

**Why the obvious number is wrong.** If you compare the 8 weeks after launch with the 8 weeks before it, sales look up **30%** (€30.5k a week vs €23.5k). That gap is almost entirely seasonal and existing growth:

- **Holiday season:** in 2024, with no redesign, the same weeks rose **27%** over the preceding 8 weeks. Every year November and December jump.
- **Existing growth:** sales were already running about **10% above the year before** from January to October 2025, before the launch.

**The adjusted estimate.** I took last year's sales for the same 8 weeks and scaled them up by the ~10% growth rate from before the launch. That predicts about €238k for 3 Nov to 28 Dec 2025. Actual sales were €244k:

| | 8 weeks after launch |
|---|---|
| Expected without redesign | ~€238.3k–238.7k |
| Actual | €244.2k |
| **Difference** | **+€5.5k–5.9k (+2.3% to 2.5%)** |

**Why it isn't conclusive.** Growth over the year before already moved by as much as ±5 points between 8-week stretches before the launch. The post-launch rise of about 2.5 points is well inside that range. Looking week by week, 7 of the 8 post-launch weeks were up 7–15% on the year before, which is the normal range. The one outlier (+24%, week of 17 Nov) is mostly because the same week in 2024 was unusually weak.

**Suggested wording for the board:** *"Sales since the redesign are up 30% on the prior two months, but that is mostly the normal holiday rise plus the ~10% growth we already had. After adjusting for both, the redesign's effect is about +2–3% (~€700 a week), which is too small to separate from normal variation after only 8 weeks."*

**Caveats:**
- This is only 8 weeks of data, and all of it falls in the holiday period.
- Nothing else that changed (marketing, prices, promotions) can be ruled out.
- A firmer answer needs either a few more months of data or conversion and traffic data from the site itself, ideally from an A/B test.
