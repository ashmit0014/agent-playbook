**Short answer: the data doesn't show a clear lift from the redesign. My best estimate is about +2%, or roughly €5k over the 8 weeks since launch, and I can't rule out zero.** A number like "+29%" would be wrong.

**Why the obvious comparison misleads**

- Average weekly sales were €23,643 in the 9 weeks before launch (Sep to Oct 2025) and €30,523 in the 8 weeks after. That's **+29%**.
- But the same weeks in 2024, before any redesign, rose **+27%** (€21,352 to €27,047). November and December are your seasonal peak every year.
- So almost all of the 29% jump is the normal holiday season.

**Adjusting for season and growth**

I compared each week to the same week a year earlier:

| Period | Sales vs. same week last year |
|---|---|
| Before launch (Jan to Oct 2025, 43 weeks) | +10.4% on average (weekly range about −1% to +24%) |
| After launch (Nov to Dec 2025, 8 weeks) | +13.0% on average |

- The business was already growing about 10% a year before the redesign. After launch, growth was about 2.4 points faster than that.
- **Estimated lift: +2.4%, or about €5,400** on €244k of sales over the 8 weeks (expected without the redesign: about €239k).
- **95% confidence interval: about −1% to +6%.** Because zero is inside that range, the lift isn't statistically significant.
- A simpler check gives a similar answer: this year's seasonal jump minus last year's comes to about +1.9%.

**What I'd tell the board**

> "Since the 3 November relaunch, sales have grown about 2% faster than our existing trend would predict, roughly €5k over 8 weeks. That's within normal week-to-week variation, so we can't yet say the redesign caused it. The big jump in November and December is mostly the usual holiday season."

**Limits**

- There are only 8 weeks of data since launch, all in peak season.
- The data has no control group, and I can't account for anything else that changed this year, such as promotions, pricing, marketing spend or holiday timing.
- Firmer answers would come from a few more months of data (especially January to March, outside peak season) and website-specific measures such as conversion rate and traffic, ideally from an A/B test.
