**Subject:** Heating repair needed at [address/unit]

Hi [Landlord's name],

The heating at [address] has been broken for four days now. As the weather gets colder, the place is becoming hard to live in, and I need this fixed as soon as possible.

Could you please arrange a repair visit within the next 48 hours? I can work around a technician's schedule, so just tell me when someone can come.

If you need anything from me first, such as access details or a description of the problem, let me know.

Thank you, and I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Best regards,
Sam

---

It's about 105 words, under your 150 limit. I counted by hand; there's no separate check.

- **What makes it firm:** it names a deadline (48 hours) and says plainly that you need it fixed. It doesn't just ask whether they could look into it.
- **What keeps it polite:** it offers to work around their schedule and to give them whatever they need, and it thanks them at the end.
- **Your call:** I chose the 48-hour deadline. Change it if a shorter or longer one suits your situation better. If you've already reported the problem by phone or text, adding "as I mentioned on [date]" to the first paragraph would make it firmer.
